---
name: Chess strength labels
description: Why the local chess opponent uses approximate difficulty levels.
---

The 700/900/1100 labels are relative difficulty tiers, not official Elo ratings. Keep that distinction clear unless a stronger calibrated engine is adopted.

**Why:** The project prioritizes a simple, low-cost, server-free static app; the engine has not been benchmarked against human opponents.

**How to apply:** When changing the engine or setup screen, preserve clear labeling and do not claim exact Elo without calibration.
